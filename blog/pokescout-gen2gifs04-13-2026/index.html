Pokedex Based Organization

Updating Pokedex gifs for Generation 2 Pokemon.

Introduction

Thanks for checking out my first blog post for this project! I was eager to get started on the project and was not too focused on documentation until now, so not everything has been documented. I decided this would be a good point to start as I am pretty proud of how far I have come so far. Thank you for checking out my project, hopefully you can find something that interests you or prompts a new idea for your own project.

New Feature

I wanted users to be able to find Pokemon in a fun and familiar way. I decided that having a replica of the Pokemon Pokedex would be easy for most Pokemon trainers to interact with. I am writing this after pushing my commit for the Gen 2 Pokemon gifs.

Extra Details

Previously, all gifs for all Pokemon in the Pokedex would load. I figured that this would scale very poorly once I push onto Azure for my cloud hosting. This is when I had the idea to only show the starter Pokemon for each generation. Then, once the user clicks on one of the starters to view the generation, it only loads the Pokemon for that generation. After they see the Pokemon for that geneartion, they can click on any of their choosing to pull up cards for that Pokemon. Card images are cached once pulled from the PokeWallet API calls.
